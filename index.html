🎙 Voice Command Reference
Basic Syntax
Say the jersey number (as digits or spoken words), then the stat. Add the game clock anywhere by saying at or time followed by the clock value.
[#] [stat]Basic entry
[#] [stat] at [mm:ss]With clock time
[#] [stat] time [mm:ss]With clock time
🔢 Jersey Numbers — Digits or Spoken Words
You can say the jersey number as digits or spell it out — both work.
"one goal"#1 Rogoff — Goal
"seven assist"#7 Adams — Assist
"twenty two goal"#22 — Goal
"twenty three save"#23 Donnelly — Save
⏱ Recording Clock Time
Say at or time followed by the game clock anywhere in the phrase — before or after the stat.
"22 goal at 8 30"#22 goal @ 8:30
"seven assist time 12"#7 assist @ 12:00
"one save at 4:20"#1 save @ 4:20
"3 ground ball at 6 45"#3 GB @ 6:45
You can also type the time in the Clock time field before speaking — it applies to the next stat logged.

🟨 Penalties
Syntax: [#] [penalty type] [duration] [at/time clock]
Duration is optional (defaults to 1 min). Clock time is optional.
"22 slash"Slashing, 1 min
"7 cross check two minute"Cross check, 2 min
"one push at 8 30"#1 pushing @ 8:30
"3 slash thirty seconds at 6"#3 slash 0:30 @ 6:00

💡 Tips
• Jersey numbers: say digits ("7 goal") or words ("seven goal") — both work. No need to say "zero seven" for single-digit numbers.
• Assists auto-link to the most recent unassisted goal
• Tap ✕ on any event log entry to undo it — the stat is removed from totals
• The score auto-increments when you log a goal. Use +/− buttons in the header to correct it
• Use the quick buttons when it's too loud for voice — they also accept the clock time field
