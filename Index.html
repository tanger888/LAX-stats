📖 Voice Commands

Say: "[#] [stat]"  ·  Optionally prefix with time: "[time] [#] [stat]"

With time: "8 30 22 goal" → #22 goal @ 8:30

Goals: "22 goal" · "22 shot" · "22 shot on goal"
Defense: "14 ground ball" · "1 save" · "9 caused turnover"
Face-offs: "3 face off win" · "3 face off loss"
Penalties: "22 slash" · "22 cross check" · "22 push" · "22 hold" · "22 crease" · "22 unsportsmanlike" · "22 expulsion"
Other: "timeout" · "opponent timeout" · "next quarter"
